# Code of conduct

BrowseWeave contributors and maintainers must keep project spaces respectful, focused, and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and defects without personal attacks, harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexualized conduct.
- Assume good faith while remaining specific about evidence and impact.
- Respect privacy. Never pressure anyone to share credentials, tokens, private browsing data, personal information, or unredacted screenshots.
- Keep security reports private until a coordinated disclosure is agreed.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, safety boundaries, moderation, and release readiness.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, intimidation, doxxing, spam, deliberate disruption, evasion of moderation, and requests to bypass CAPTCHA, anti-bot systems, access controls, or other security protections are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary. Do not report conduct problems in a public issue if doing so would expose personal information. Use GitHub's private content-reporting or abuse-reporting controls, or contact the repository owner privately through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed on a best-effort basis without retaliation against good-faith reporters.

This code applies in the repository and in other public spaces where someone is representing BrowseWeave.
